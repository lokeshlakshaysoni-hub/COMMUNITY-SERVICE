About Bharat Nursery, Pataudi

Our history, sustainable community values, digital presence, physical location, and visitor feedback.

Serving the Pataudi Community Since 2014

Bharat Nursery was founded with a singular mission: to make regional gardening successful, sustainable, and free of synthetic chemical pesticides. Located in the fertile belt of Pataudi (Haryana), we specialize in growing acclimatized native Indian saplings that easily withstand local hot summers, dry winters, and sudden monsoon showers.

Rather than importing weak greenhouse-grown plants that wither within days of purchase, our saplings are raised in open natural conditions. We educate our visitors on correct potting mix ratios, watering discipline, and non-toxic organic pest management using cold-pressed neem formulations.

Core Promise: We never sell a plant without first verifying whether your home environment has the appropriate sunlight and space for it to thrive.
Main Grounds: Acclimatization Polyhouse & Organic Vermicompost Yard, Pataudi.

Acclimatization Shade House

50% agro-shade net shelter where saplings gradually adjust to outdoor sunlight before distribution.

Vermicomposting Unit

In-house organic waste recycling producing pure earthworm compost rich in essential micronutrients.

Native Seed Preservation

Seed-bank conserving rare traditional varieties of Tulsi, Desi Rose, Peepal, and indigenous shade trees.
