Primary Core Focus

UN Sustainable Development Goal 11: Sustainable Cities & Communities

Urban concrete density in the NCR region leads to severe microclimatic heat islands and critical air quality deterioration. Bharat Nursery’s digital platform is fundamentally structured to address SDG Target 11.7: providing universal access to safe, inclusive, and green domestic and public spaces.

SDG Target 11.7 - Urban Green Spaces

Balcony & Terrace Canopy Expansion

Eliminates the 40% domestic plant mortality rate through microclimate compatibility matching, turning bare urban balconies into productive photosynthetic canopies.

SDG Target 11.6 - Urban Environmental Impact

Airborne Particulate (PM2.5) Filtration

Promotes high surface-area foliage (Neem, Ashoka, Peepal) that traps airborne particulate pollutants, drastically improving immediate household ambient air quality.

SDG Target 11.B - Disaster Risk & Resilience

Urban Microclimate Heat Island Reduction

Strategically positioned vegetative shade on residential terraces lowers building cooling thermal loads by up to 3 degrees Celsius during peak summer.

Auxiliary Environmental Support: Primary: SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities) SDG 12 (Plastic-Free Coir Pots) SDG 13 (High Carbon Sequestering Trees) SDG 15 (Native Biodiversity Corridors)

Why Bharat Nursery Outperforms Commercial Markets

Unlike commercial nurseries that prioritize fast turnover, Bharat Nursery focuses on plant survival, soil biology, and zero plastic waste.

Native Resilience

100% Acclimatized Flora

Raised in open natural fields in Pataudi rather than temperature-controlled artificial greenhouses, ensuring saplings survive in domestic yards without transplant shock.

Zero Plastic

Biodegradable Coir Root-Bags

Distributed in 100% biodegradable coconut coir planters and woven cloth root-bags that can be planted directly into the soil without disturbing root hairs.

Expert Match

Microclimate Guidance

We assess your balcony direction, sunlight hours, and seasonal winds before recommending plants, eliminating plant mortality.

Soil Health

Enriched Organic Vermicompost

Every plant is potted in rich, chemical-free organic vermicompost, cow dung manure, and neem cake to naturally resist root fungus.
